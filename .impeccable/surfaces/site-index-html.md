# Landing page (GitHub Pages)

Scope: the public site at farhans-codes.github.io/ai_limit_status. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: developers on paid Claude Code or Codex plans, on Mac or Windows,
who fear hitting a limit mid-task. Action: download for their platform (or
brew install). Proof: the live board demonstrating the product's numbers and
reset times; the real macOS screenshot; open source and no telemetry.
Constraints: no invented users, ratings or claims; label demo data as sample;
Not-affiliated disclaimer in the footer; English only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Usage limits read like a station departure board: each provider is a
service, each reset a scheduled time, macOS and Windows are the two platforms
you board from. Refuses the dark-gradient hero with a screenshot beside a
headline and a feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Black board housing with split-flap tiles in flap yellow (#f2c14e)
and enamel white, a hairline split across every tile; station enamel signage
(off-white enamel, black lettering, rounded plate corners) for everything below
the board; signal red only for a low limit. Condensed signage grotesque for
flaps and headings, its regular cut for reading text; tabular digits
throughout.

STORY: The visitor sees their own situation on the board within a second
(Codex 68%, Claude 58%, resets Thu and Fri), reads that it lives in the menu
bar or taskbar on both platforms, picks their platform sign, and finds setup is
just signing in to the CLI they already use.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width departure board fills the top: a header strip
with the product name and a live local clock, then rows flipping to Provider,
Limit, Remaining, Resets. Above the rows, the headline set as the board's
title. Directly under the board, two enamel platform signs side by side:
"Platform 1 · macOS · Download DMG" and "Platform 2 · Windows · Download
installer", with the brew command beneath.

FORM: Departure board, candidate 1 of 7 on my ordered list (impeccable's pick,
chosen by the user); seed key c9987077. Signature interaction: on load the
board's flaps riffle through characters and settle on their values, row by
row; reduced motion shows final values at once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
