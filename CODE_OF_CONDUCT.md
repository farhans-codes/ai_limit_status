# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AI Limit Status welcoming and safe
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private information, especially account and credential data.
- Give credit and respect licenses and trademarks.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation.
- Requesting or sharing provider credentials or access tokens.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/farhans-codes). Do not include
sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as
promptly and confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and to public representation of
the project or its community.
